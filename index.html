01 /WHO WE ARE

Technology is better when curiosity meets responsibility.

Black Shadow Team is an independent technology-focused team founded by Musfiqur Rahim in 2022. Its public presence and publishing expanded in 2025–2026.

We explore emerging technology, make learning approachable, and turn thoughtful experiments into useful digital work. Openness, creativity, and responsible practice guide the team.
