01 /WHO WE ARE

Technology is better when curiosity meets responsibility.

Black Shadow Team is an independent, technology-focused team founded by Musfiqur Rahim. Since 2022, we’ve been growing our ideas and work; our public presence expanded in 2025–2026.

We’re here to explore emerging technology, make learning approachable, and turn thoughtful experiments into useful digital work. We value openness, creativity, and responsible practice.
