01 /WHO WE ARE

Technology is better when curiosity meets responsibility.

Black Shadow Team is an independent, technology-focused team founded by Musfiqur Rahim. Founded in 2022, our public presence expanded in 2025–2026.

We explore emerging technology, make learning approachable, and turn thoughtful experiments into useful digital work. We value openness, creativity, and responsible practice.
